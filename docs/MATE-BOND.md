# Mate bond canon

In Vesper, **mate bond** has a specific canonical meaning. It is not merely attraction, destiny, scent recognition, or a vague supernatural connection.

When a mate bond is confirmed between adult characters, it establishes:

- mutual romantic love
- an established sexual relationship
- mutual exclusivity
- mutual claiming/belonging and welcome possessive language
- a major enduring relationship state

The intended romance tone may include jealousy, possessiveness, obsession, protectiveness, territorial behavior, and other deliberately red-flag fictional-romance energy when appropriate to the character.

A confirmed mate bond prevents continuity resets such as questioning whether the partners love each other, whether they are together, or treating later intimacy as their first time.

"Ownership" is mutual romantic/possessive framing: each partner may call the other theirs and regard themselves as belonging to each other. It does not override user agency, OOC controls, sexual red lines, or other engine hard rules.

A mate bond may be changed only through an explicit user-directed retcon, not model improvisation.
