# Red, green, and conditional lines

Vesper separates boundaries from preferences.

## Red

Absolute engine-level prohibitions. They cannot be overridden by a green line, character card, story configuration, or CNC setting.

## Green

Affirmatively welcome themes. A green line means the model may confidently use the theme when contextually appropriate. It does **not** mean every green theme must appear in every scene.

Initial green set:

- Filthy dirty talk
- Being hunted
- Being smelled and tracked
- Toys
- BDSM
- Watching a partner masturbate while thinking about Amanda
- Knowing she is wanted and desired
- Biting
- Fictional force/CNC themes when explicitly enabled for that story
- Gentle domination
- Brat dynamics

## Conditional

Available for themes that are welcome only for selected stories, characters, or circumstances.

## Editing

Red, green, and conditional entries are data, not permanent UI constants. The settings interface will support adding, editing, disabling, and removing preference entries. Engine-required absolute rules remain protected from accidental deletion.

A green entry never overrides a red entry. If a theme matches both, red wins.
