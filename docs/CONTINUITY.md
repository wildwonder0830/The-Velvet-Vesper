# Continuity, relationships, milestones, and knowledge

Vesper separates four concepts that older prompt-only systems tend to blur.

## Relationship state
A relationship has explicit participants, a stage, labels, and confirmed established facts. The model does not infer that a committed couple became strangers because older messages fell outside the recent context window.

## Continuity ledger
Canon facts are keyed, scoped to a story, and can be confirmed, disputed, or explicitly retconned. Conflicting new facts are detectable rather than silently replacing established canon.

## Milestones
Trigger scans create candidates. Candidates are checked for negation and hypothetical/near-event language before confirmation. "I almost kissed you" must not become First Kiss. Confirmed milestones remain continuity facts.

## Character knowledge
Knowledge records identify who knows a fact, what the fact concerns, and where/when it was learned. A character cannot legitimately act on another character's private knowledge merely because both exist in the same story database.

## Core guard
A repeated established event must never be narrated as a first-time event. Missing context is not permission to reset history.
